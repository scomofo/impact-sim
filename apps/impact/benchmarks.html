Read this comparison correctly

These are selected outputs from Collins, Melosh & Marcus (2005), Table 6—not measurements of actual impacts. Agreement checks implementation consistency; it does not establish overall scientific accuracy.

The 3% display tolerance is a review aid for rounded references, not a scientific uncertainty bound. Positive differences mean the model is higher. Missing values are not zero.
